Selection details

Measure definitions

Density uses distinct ties or directed connections, with no self-links. Isolates stay in the population. Weak components ignore arrow direction; strong components require paths both ways. Clustering is the average local coefficient of the undirected projection, including zeros. Reciprocity is the share of directed connections with a reverse connection. Reachable pairs are ordered source–destination pairs. These structural counts do not use distances, after applying any weight exclusions.

These settings apply to all analysis tools. Your diagram data stays unchanged.

Relationship layers

Parallel records use the strongest available connection (minimum cost). Frequency zero is excluded. Confidence is never a numeric weight. Weighted harmonic scores have distance-dependent units and may exceed 1.

Start and end are inclusive. One missing boundary is treated as open-ended. Last verified is evidence metadata, not a relationship's start or end.

How communities are detected

Deterministic greedy modularity merges groups while a merge improves modularity at the selected resolution. Higher resolution generally favors smaller groups. Flow mode uses an undirected projection; mutual and parallel records use the strongest affinity per pair. For explicit path costs, affinity is 1 / cost. Isolates remain separate. Rings mark algorithmic communities; circle fill colors retain your assigned groups. Results describe structural clusters, not confirmed affiliations.

Effective size measures nonredundant contacts; efficiency divides effective size by degree. Lower constraint indicates less concentration in interconnected contacts. These describe recorded network structure, not personal influence.

Calculation conventions

Uses the undirected projection of included connections and the strongest affinity per pair. Explicit path costs use 1 / cost. Effective size = Σⱼ(1 − Σq pᵢq mⱼq); p normalizes weights by their sum, m by the contact's maximum weight. Constraint = Σⱼ(pᵢⱼ + Σq pᵢq pqⱼ)². Constraint can exceed 1. Isolates are undefined, shown as N/A.

A k-core contains people with at least k connections to others remaining within it. This uses distinct undirected connections after the current exclusions; numerical weights do not change the degree requirement.

How this works

Connector score is normalized betweenness centrality. It measures the share of shortest routes between other people that pass through this person, sharing credit when routes tie. Direct contacts count distinct neighbors.
